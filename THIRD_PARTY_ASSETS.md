# Third-party visual assets

## Level evolution lightning

- Files: `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/level_strike_01.png` through `level_strike_22.png`
- Work: **2D Spell Effects** (`fx1_blue_topEffect`)
- Creator: Mikodrak
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/2d-spell-effects
- License: CC0 1.0 / public domain dedication
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source package states that the effect was prepared with particle animation in Blender and processed in After Effects. The PNG frame sequence is used with its transparent background; the original artwork itself was not redrawn.
